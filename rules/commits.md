# Commit messages

1. Subject and body are separated by a blank line.
2. Subject up to 50 characters. Without a type prefix it starts with a capital letter; after
   `<type>: ` the text starts lowercase.
3. No period at the end of the subject.
4. Imperative mood: "add", "fix", not "added", "fixes".
5. Body wrapped at 72 characters.
6. Body says what changed and why, not how.
7. Types are the branch types: feat, fix, refactor, docs, test, chore, exp.
8. One logical change per commit; during review, fixes are separate commits, never squashed.
